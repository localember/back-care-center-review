What to expect
The short answer

Wellness care is ongoing chiropractic care after the original problem has settled, for people who would rather keep their spine and joints moving well than wait for the next flare-up. It is a personal choice, not a medical requirement, and the honest version of that matters: we will lay out what it can and cannot do, suggest a sensible pace, and never pressure you into an open-ended commitment.

Ongoing care is where chiropractic gets the most criticism, and often fairly, because it is sometimes sold as a lifelong necessity it is not. Here is the grounded version: what it is, who tends to value it, and where we draw the line.

What is wellness care?

Once an acute problem has calmed down, some people stop, and that is completely reasonable. Others find that occasional, ongoing care helps them stay comfortable and moving the way they want to, the same way people keep up exercise or regular dental visits. Wellness care is simply that: periodic visits chosen to maintain how you feel and move, rather than to fix a specific complaint.

It is a choice, not a diagnosis, and we approach it that way. The right amount is the amount that earns its place for you, and we will say so plainly if we do not think you need to keep coming.

Who tends to value it?

It suits people whose day-to-day life is hard on their body and who would rather stay ahead of trouble than react to it.

  • People with physically demanding jobs or long hours at a desk.
  • Active people and athletes who want to keep training comfortably.
  • Anyone whose old problem tends to creep back when they let things slide.
  • People who simply feel and move better with occasional, ongoing care.

And to be clear about the other side: if you feel great and have no reason to keep coming, you do not have to. We would rather you trust that than feel talked into a schedule.

Is ongoing care necessary?

For most people, honestly, no. It is a preference, not a medical necessity, and we will not dress it up as one. What we can say is that some people genuinely feel and function better with periodic care and choose it for that reason, the same way they choose to keep exercising. Our job is to give you a straight picture so the choice is yours, made on good information.

What does a plan look like?

Usually a light, spaced-out rhythm rather than frequent visits: a check-in every so often, adjusted to how you are doing and what your body asks for. We review it honestly as we go, dial it back when it is not adding much, and never lock you into a long block up front.

What does it cost, and do you take insurance?

Most insurance covers care for an active problem rather than ongoing wellness visits, so wellness care is often out of pocket. We will always be clear about the numbers up front so you can decide whether it is worth it to you.

  • No referral needed, and no obligation to continue.
  • We are clear that wellness visits are usually not covered by insurance.
  • Simple, up-front pricing, and an honest word if we think you do not need to keep coming.

We cannot promise a specific outcome, and ongoing care is a personal choice rather than a medical necessity. What we can promise is a straight picture of what it does and does not do, a sensible pace, and no pressure to commit to more than you want.
