What to expect
The short answer

Laser therapy uses focused light energy, applied through a handpiece over the skin, to warm the tissue and support the body's own recovery in a sore or irritated area. It is drug-free, non-invasive, and painless, and in our office it is used alongside chiropractic care rather than on its own. It suits some problems well and is not a cure-all, and we will tell you honestly which is which.

Laser therapy gets marketed with big promises. Here is the grounded version: what it actually is, how a session feels, where we find it useful, and an honest word on what the evidence does and does not show.

What is therapeutic laser?

It is a device that delivers specific wavelengths of light into the tissue, a process often called photobiomodulation. The light is absorbed by the cells in the area and is thought to support their normal recovery activity and ease local irritation. You feel a gentle warmth and nothing sharp; there are no needles and no medication involved.

We use it as one tool among several, aimed at the same problem your care plan is aimed at, rather than as a standalone answer.

Who is it for?

We tend to reach for it when a specific area is irritated or slow to settle and someone wants a gentle, drug-free option to support the rest of their care.

  • A localised ache or strain that is slow to calm down.
  • Soft-tissue irritation around a joint we are also working on.
  • Recovery support for active people between harder efforts.
  • People who would rather try a non-invasive option before anything stronger.

It is not right for every situation, and there are areas and conditions where we would not use it. A short check before we begin settles whether it is a sensible fit for you.

Is laser therapy proven to work?

Honestly, the evidence is mixed and still developing. For some kinds of localised pain and soft-tissue irritation it appears to help a meaningful number of people, and for others the picture is less clear. We offer it as a reasonable option to try for the right problem, not as a guarantee, and we would rather be straight with you than oversell it.

What does a session involve?

You rest comfortably while the handpiece is moved over the area for a few minutes. Most people feel a pleasant warmth and nothing more, and there is no downtime afterwards, so you carry on with your day. We usually pair it with the hands-on care and movement that address why the area got overloaded in the first place.

What does it cost, and do you take insurance?

Coverage for laser therapy varies by plan and is often not covered. We are happy to check your benefits for you first and give you clear numbers before anything begins.

  • No referral needed to book a first visit.
  • We verify your benefits for you ahead of time, so you know what to expect.
  • Clear, up-front pricing if you are paying out of pocket, with no packages you did not ask for.

We cannot promise a specific outcome or what your insurance will cover, and laser therapy is not right for every case. What we can promise is a gentle, drug-free approach, an honest read on whether it fits, and a straight answer either way.
